Delete your PurePlate account

This page explains how to delete your account and what is removed.

Option 1: in the app (patients, fastest)

  1. Open PurePlate and log in.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your password and tap Delete.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address, phone number and password hash.
  • All of your appointments.
  • All of your diet charts, including the image files.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone. We keep no copy of this data. Hosting providers may keep routine server logs, such as IP addresses, for their standard periods.

Option 2: by email

Use this if you forgot your password, no longer have the app, or have a provider account. Email us from the address you registered with and we will delete the account and its data within 14 days. We may reply to confirm that the account is yours.

Email a deletion request

Or write to haridevapps.support@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my PurePlate account".
