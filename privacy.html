PurePlate privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026

PurePlate is a demonstration project, not a medical service. Please do not enter real medical or personal health information.

Who we are

PurePlate is a mobile app built by Haridev. Patients book appointments with dietitians, receive diet charts and join video consultations. You can contact us at haridevapps.support@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, optional phone number and password. The password is stored only as a one-way hash, so we cannot read it.
  • Appointments: the provider, date and time, and status of each booking.
  • Diet charts: images that a provider uploads for a patient, with optional notes.
  • Video calls: audio and video travel between the people on the call through the Agora service. PurePlate does not record or store calls.
  • Technical data: our hosting providers may log IP addresses and request details for security and troubleshooting.

PurePlate does not include advertising or analytics tools, does not track you across other apps, and does not collect your location or contacts.

Device permissions

  • Camera and microphone: used only during a video call.
  • Photo library: used only when a provider chooses an image to upload as a diet chart.

How we use your data

We use your data only to run the app: signing you in, managing appointments, delivering diet charts, connecting video calls and keeping the service secure. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Services we rely on

  • Amazon Web Services: hosting, and private file storage for diet chart images in the Asia Pacific (Mumbai) region.
  • MongoDB Atlas: the database that holds accounts and appointments.
  • Agora: real-time video and audio for calls.

Each of these providers handles data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Security

The app connects to our servers over HTTPS. Diet chart images sit in a private storage bucket and are opened only through links that expire after five minutes. Access is limited to the patient and the provider concerned. No system is perfectly secure, which is one reason this is a demonstration app.

How long we keep data, and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. Patients can delete their account inside the app (Settings, then Delete account). This immediately and permanently deletes the profile, all appointments, and all diet charts including their image files. Provider accounts are removed on request by email. Files that were uploaded but never attached to a chart, for example when an upload is interrupted, are not linked to any account and may remain until they are cleaned up. Hosting providers keep their own server logs for their standard periods. See how to delete your account.

Your choices

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it by emailing haridevapps.support@gmail.com.

Children

PurePlate is not intended for anyone under 18.

Not medical advice

PurePlate does not provide medical advice and is not for emergencies. If you think you have a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above.
